Cripple Creek KOA Holiday

Privacy

This website provides public information about Cripple Creek KOA Holiday. It does not provide a guest account, reservation checkout, payment form, or campground inventory system. When you choose an external booking or information link, you leave this site and use the linked provider's website under that provider's privacy practices.

Information you provide

This site does not ask visitors to submit names, reservation numbers, payment details, or other guest information through a form. If you contact the campground by phone or email, the campground may receive the information you choose to send and may use it to respond to your request.

Links and third-party services

The site links to official KOA pages and external campground resources. Those sites may use their own cookies, analytics, booking tools, or privacy policies. Review the applicable provider notice before submitting information or completing a reservation.

Updates and questions

This privacy page describes the current behavior of this public information site. If the site's functionality changes, this page should be reviewed and updated. For campground questions, use the contact page; for reservations, use the official KOA reservation page.
