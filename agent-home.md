# Cripple Creek KOA Holiday

Cripple Creek KOA Holiday is a campground near Cripple Creek, Colorado, at about 10,000 feet. The property offers RV sites, cabins, deluxe cabins, and tent sites, with mountain views, pet-friendly options, Wi-Fi, showers, laundry, a dog park, pizza, propane, firewood, and EV charging.
