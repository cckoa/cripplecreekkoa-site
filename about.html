About Cripple Creek KOA Holiday

Cripple Creek KOA Holiday is a seasonal campground near Cripple Creek, Colorado. The campground sits at approximately 10,000 feet and provides a mountain setting for travelers exploring the historic Cripple Creek and Victor area, Mueller State Park, Florissant Fossil Beds, scenic drives, and the wider Pikes Peak region.

Guests can choose among RV sites, cabins, deluxe cabins, and tent sites, subject to current availability. The property information published on this site describes amenities including Wi-Fi, showers, laundry, a dog park, a camp store, pizza service, propane, firewood, and EV charging. Pet-friendly options are available, and guests should confirm current pet rules and site details before booking.

This site is a concise information guide for trip planning. The official KOA listing remains the source for current reservations, rates, availability, operating details, and campground policies. The campground address is 2576 County Road 81, Cripple Creek, Colorado 80813.
